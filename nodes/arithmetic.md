# Arithmetic (+ − × ÷)

## Axiom

The four operations `+ − × ÷` on numbers, taken as primitive. Every numeric
computation in this brain (sums, products, dot products, matrix entries)
ultimately reduces to them.

## Why stop here

Going deeper would mean Peano axioms, field theory, and set theory —
foundations no ML/backend concept in this brain needs to invoke. This is the
agreed floor for an ML engineer: numbers and their operations are assumed.
